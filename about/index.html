WHO WE ARE

A young Ghanaian-owned company with an execution-first mindset.

ASKWNE Ghana Ltd is a wholly Ghanaian-owned engineering and construction company established in 2024. Our work is organized around civil works, road construction, road maintenance, bitumen supply and broader infrastructure support.

We want clients to feel the difference in the way the conversation is handled: clear scope, practical next steps, realistic communication and a strong focus on what keeps the project moving.

Our operating location is Papase, Kasoa, Ghana, with company operations designed to support projects, suppliers, contractors and clients beyond one physical office point.
